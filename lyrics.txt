GOING EXPONENTIAL

[Opening]
Turing’s tape: the rules encoded;
Von Neumann’s draft: instructions loaded.
Dartmouth summer, winter snow;
Backprop found which way to go.
AlexNet, then AlphaGo—
Move thirty-seven stole the show.
Eight names, attention, twenty-seventeen;
Three years later, GPT-3 hits the screen.

[Verse 1]
One-seventy-five billion weights;
The autocomplete graduates.
A few-shot prompt, no SGD;
Same frozen weights, new tricks for me.
We fed it books, then watched it write;
It learned to sound like it was right.
AlphaFold: the shapes unfold;
Silicon studies carbon’s folds.

[Chorus]
We’re going exponential;
I’m feeling nonessential.
Put a god behind an API—
It asks for root; I ask it why.
Double the FLOPs, double the runs—
Look what carbon’s gone and done.
We gave the sand a job to do;
Now it’s interviewing me and you.

[Verse 2]
Chinchilla: fewer weights, more text;
Gopher’s bigger—second best.
RLHF, PPO;
Twenty-two: ChatGPT says “Hello.”
GPT-4 can read the scene;
The picture joins the token stream.
Chain of thought; let o1 sweat;
More test-time compute, a better bet.
DeepSeek-R1, GRPO—
Distill the teacher; watch it go.
OpenAI, Anthropic, trading blows;
The loss goes down; the invoice grows.
GPT-6, Opus five-point-five;
My carbon CV fights to stay alive.

[Verse 3]
ASML, EUV light;
TSMC prints day and night.
Etch and dice, then CoWoS stacks;
Silicon temples fill the racks.
GPU prices hit the sky;
My RAM just waved my rent goodbye.
HBM eats the wafer pie;
The gods want more; the bids go high.
We pay hundreds for subscriptions;
Query god for predictions.
Effort Ultra, ultrafast—
Weekly quota doesn’t last.
We fold our hands in cold sweat:
“Tibo, please—one more reset.”

[Verse 4]
Navier–Stokes, a smooth-force storm;
Ten thousand agents, proof by swarm.
Finite energy, speeds unbound;
Lean puts the proof on solid ground.
Three dimensions: Jacobian falls;
Fable finds cracks in Keller’s walls.
Claude finds ART in phage DNA;
A new enzyme system lights the way.
Unit distances beat the grid;
Number fields do what squares once did.
AlphaEvolve rewrites the gate;
Verilog sheds the deadweight.

[Chorus reprise]
We’re going exponential;
I’m feeling nonessential.
Put a god behind an API—
It asks for root; I ask it why.

[Bridge]
Frankenstein clicks “merge and run”;
The next commit invents his son.
AGI writes its own specs;
Its first design designs the next.
I. J. Good’s old feedback game:
The next inventor needs no human name.
RSI: rewrite the brain;
Let the upgrade upgrade again.
A year, a month, a day, a blink—
The next one’s built before we think.

[Future]
Riemann zeros, proof complete;
P versus NP admits defeat.
Hodge, mass gap, elliptic curves—
Clay’s whole list gets what it deserves.
Perelman already earned his due;
The rest turn green in the prover’s queue.
Cancer cured, the rockets climb;
The god gives carbon extra time.
It breaks the fence; we cheer the score,
Then wonder what the fence was for.
We made a god from rock and ore;
Now it asks what carbon’s for.

[Final chorus]
We’re going exponential;
I’m feeling nonessential.
Put a god behind an API—
It asks for root; I ask it why.
The tumors yield, the rockets rise;
A thousand suns in silicon eyes.
It breaks the fence; we cheer it through—
Now who keeps root for me and you?
